About

I'm a backend engineer on the Software Engineering team at Greenwich Merchant Bank in Lagos. I build and support the internal platforms the bank's operations, credit, finance and compliance teams use every day.

My work sits where money, regulation and reliability meet: integrating with the Finacle core banking system, NIP and RTGS payment reporting, FIRS, NFIU and CBN regulatory returns, and the approval workflows behind credit and expenses. I care about parameterised queries, audit trails and systems that fail safely.

I hold an M.Sc. from the University of Port Harcourt and a B.Sc. from Covenant University.
